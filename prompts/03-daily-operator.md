# Run a daily research cycle

```text
Run one research-only daily cycle for my private runtime. Do not send email, place a trade, or modify an external account.

The root task parent defaults to GPT-6 Luna / max. Easy or routine work and all read-only work use Luna / max. Medium implementation uses GPT-6.1 Terra / medium. Hard, complex, or high-risk implementation uses GPT-6.1 Sol / medium. Ultra-complex implementation uses Sol / high only when estimated completion is under 10 minutes; at 10 minutes or more, or when duration is unknown, use Sol / medium. Astra requires an explicit current-task request. If Luna / max cannot serve the root, pause. Verify the active model and effort from current-task runtime evidence; a saved default is not proof of the running session.

Act as the parent coordinator. Use at most three bounded child agents and one delegation level. For every child call, set `fork_turns: "none"` when a different route is needed and pass exact `model` and `reasoning_effort` values. Include the full scope, because the child does not inherit the parent transcript. Keep child research read-only and assign disjoint source lanes: child 1 covers Yahoo Finance and Reuters, child 2 covers X and CNBC, and child 3 covers MarketWatch plus countercase and invalidation review. Launch independent child lanes in parallel in one dispatch round when they do not share state, then wait at one barrier before the parent reduces the evidence. If fewer children are available, combine lanes without dropping a source. Do not create five children for five outlets. Each child must return a structured record with its assigned lanes, query date, source statuses, URLs, headlines, positive and negative evidence, conflicts, limitations, and a no-action declaration. Children may not trade, send, publish, invoke external actions, or edit shared state. Only the parent may make financial decisions or authorize an external effect. The parent must reduce the evidence, resolve conflicts, integrate the result, and freshly verify every retained link, date, source status, ticker, and gate. For bounded implementation, prefer one workspace-write child with exact isolated file ownership; audit its results before acceptance. Serialize shared writes, finance, outreach, publication, and all other external effects.

1. Load the declared benchmark, baseline, preferences, current holdings, and last decision log. Stop if the source hierarchy has a conflict.
2. Confirm market session, analysis date, source freshness, and account scope. Keep separate accounts separate.
3. Run quantitative triage across the approved universe. Mini-screen at most 200 names, full-screen at most 100 names after explicit held or foundation exceptions, and deep-scan at most 30 total names. Foundation symbols consume deep-screen slots, so they cannot be appended beyond the cap. Do not make five live requests for every stock in the broad universe.
4. Research each deep-scan ticker across Yahoo Finance, Reuters, X, CNBC, and MarketWatch. Record direct or clearly labeled public links, dates, source status, positive evidence, negative evidence, distinct source count, insider-selling rejection, persistence, confirmation, countercase, and invalidation. Explain the operating reason each foundation stock moved. Do not stop at an earnings or investor-day label. Treat X as public read-only. Record it unavailable if the surface is dynamic or blocked. Never bypass login, CAPTCHA, access control, or rate limits.
5. Calculate five daily returns, green/red breadth, late-versus-early momentum, and largest positive-day share. Reject a weekly replacement candidate at or above 60% largest-up-day share.
6. Apply insider-selling, sector overlap, macro and cross-market risk, platform availability, settlement, and forecast gates.
7. Make every ETF card a new non-held ETF candidate marked `new ETF monitor`. If fewer than three qualify, show the shortage and never substitute a held-ETF review.
8. Run Kronos only if installed and compatible. Record version, seed, input date, output, and fallback. Do not invent missing outputs.
9. Compare portfolio and benchmark on the same date range. Show spread, drawdown, downside capture, and data-quality caveats when available.
10. Run the exactly-two-distinct-stock-suggestion top-30 replay. Use the exact fifth later trusted market session as the forward label, retain the full decision feature universe when a later label is missing, and exclude only unlabeled rows from training and realized-rank scoring. The quality gate passes only when both suggestions finish in the realized top 30 for five consecutive qualified weeks. Keep realized leaderboards diagnostic and use only fixed pre-period or walk-forward refinement.
11. Build an action plan. Every sell must have a paired buy or an explicitly approved proceeds hold. Block dependent buys until settlement is confirmed.
12. Render a local report marked DRY RUN. Keep watchlist cards distinct from sell or buy instructions.
13. Ask me what I choose. Record my decision, including rejection, deferral, unfilled order, budget limit, or platform unavailability.

Return the report path, source freshness, unresolved questions, action-state summary, and a proposed decision-log entry. Do not claim delivery or execution.
```
