## Representative failures and what changed

### Feature and source expansion

Dated macro, filing, short-volume, and company-fact overlays were added in isolated branches. A source was eligible only when its observation or conservative release time preceded the decision. More features improved the information map, but the tested routes did not sustain two correct picks for five weeks. Some public feeds can revise past records, so a present archive is not automatically a historical vintage.

### Candidate retrieval versus pair ranking

**Retrieval** asks whether both later winners reached the candidate pool. **Pair ranking** asks whether the model selected those two from the pool. The September 7 audit found many weeks where both winners entered the top 65 but the pair stage still missed them. That separates a ranking failure from a universe-width failure. Widening a diagnostic pool cannot authorize an unapproved Cash App pick.

### Market-type stability

Defensive, growth-like, oil-like, and transition are diagnostic market-type groups. The five-week goal still requires stable results across the specified routes and groups. The September 21 upper-bound audit found maximum feasible runs of five, four, six, and three for those groups on its approved working list. The growth-like and transition groups could not satisfy a five-window run on that dated boundary even with a perfect selector (SRC-010).

### Forecasting experiments

Kronos and TimesFM 3 estimate future paths; they do not replace the exact-pair replay. The TimesFM 3 diagnostic used an explicit non-production opt-in and did not show a validated improvement over protected v14. A successfully loaded model or finite forecast is a runtime check, not a promotion result (SRC-018).

## Rules retained after failed branches

1. Keep the Cash App-approved top-65 selection scope and the realized full-market top-30 label.
2. Select exactly two distinct, non-proxy approved names to form a qualifying pair. Treat scarcity and five extra full-universe names as diagnostics until approval is established.
3. Train and choose a strategy only with labels available strictly before the decision.
4. Compare candidate and protected baseline on identical windows and report route, market-type, violations, and longest streak.
5. Keep the five-consecutive-qualified-week promotion gate unchanged.
