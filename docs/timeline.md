## How to read this history

The portfolio checkpoints measure an account comparison. The historical selector replay measures whether two research picks landed in a later full-market top 30. They have different denominators and cannot be merged into one success rate. A diagnostic result does not replace protected v14, and an archived report is not proof of a trade or email receipt.
