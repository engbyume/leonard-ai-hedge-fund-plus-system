# Limitations and next steps

The entries below distinguish recorded evidence from work still needed. They do not authorize a trade, data purchase, provider access, email, or publication.

| Limitation | Consequence | Next evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical universe and platform eligibility | Dated ETF-holdings proxies and a present-day working list cannot establish exact historical index membership or platform availability. | A source with appropriate dated membership and eligibility evidence, or an explicitly labeled proxy study. |
| Price and label coverage | Missing sessions, incomplete labels, and unverified provider rights limit a historical comparison. | A permitted source, point-in-time coverage and adjustment checks, and aligned decision and label sessions. |
| Exact-pair accuracy | Protected v14 recorded three qualifying windows out of 106, longest streak one, and `pass=false`. | A frozen candidate evaluated against the baseline on identical dates and the full stated contract. |
| Portfolio benchmark evidence | Incomplete starting-baseline and cash-flow information limits claims of an investment edge. | Comparable returns and documented treatment of cash flows, dividends, costs, and cash. |
| Report and delivery | A preview, an archived report, a sent label, and the received content are different evidence. The recorded September report did not reach delivery. | A current source-valid report, inspected render, authorized delivery, and external content readback. |
| Sector and ETF research | A stock-selection result does not prove one-month sector or ETF ranking accuracy. | A separate dated universe, holdings and price inputs, a frozen evaluation protocol, baselines, and an untouched holdout. |

The [evaluation page](evaluation-and-results.md), [progress narrative](../evidence/progress.md), and [source register](../evidence/source-register.md) provide the dated evidence behind these limits. A provider's advertised coverage is not proof that an account, license, or specific data set meets the project's requirements.
