## Reading path

1. Read the [system map](system-map.md) to see the research loop and approval boundary.
2. Read the [timeline](timeline.md) and [model history](model-history.md) for context.
3. Read [evaluation and results](evaluation-and-results.md) and [successes and failures](successes-and-failures.md) before interpreting any claim.
4. Use [limitations and next steps](limitations-and-next-steps.md) to see what remains unproven.

This is educational research, not financial advice. A watchlist, forecast, report, or backtest does not authorize a trade or email.
